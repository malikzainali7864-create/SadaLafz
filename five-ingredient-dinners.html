Five-ingredient dinners for busy weeknights

By Nadia Farooq · August 30, 2026 · 4 min read

The best weeknight recipe isn't the most impressive one — it's the one you'll actually make after a nine-hour day. These six dinners use five ingredients or fewer, not counting salt, oil and pepper, and none takes longer than 30 minutes.

1. Garlic butter pasta with greens

Toss cooked pasta with melted butter, sliced garlic, a handful of spinach wilted in the pan, and grated parmesan. Finish with cracked pepper.

2. Sheet-pan chicken and vegetables

Toss chicken thighs and chopped vegetables — whatever's in the fridge — with oil and a spice mix of your choice. Roast at 200°C for 25 minutes.

3. Tomato and egg curry

Sauté onions until soft, add chopped tomatoes and a spoon of curry paste, simmer until thick, then crack eggs directly into the sauce and cover until set. Serve with bread or rice.

4. Chickpea and spinach stew

Simmer canned chickpeas, chopped tomatoes and a handful of spinach with cumin and garlic for 15 minutes. A pantry staple that costs very little and reheats well.

5. Honey soy salmon

Whisk honey, soy sauce and grated ginger, pour over salmon fillets, and bake at 200°C for 12 minutes. Serve with steamed rice.

6. Loaded baked potatoes

Bake potatoes until soft, split them open, and top with beans, cheese and a spoon of yoghurt or sour cream.

A formula is more useful long-term than any single recipe.

The formula behind all six

Every one of these follows the same shape: one protein or base (pasta, chicken, eggs, chickpeas, salmon, potato), one vegetable, one sauce or seasoning, and pantry staples you already have. Once you see the pattern, you can build a new dinner from whatever's left in the fridge on a Thursday, without opening a recipe app at all.
